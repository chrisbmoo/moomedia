Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

moomedia Australia (“moomedia”, “we”, “us”) is a marketing, design and media agency at Studio 10, 28-31 Industrial Place, Breakwater VIC 3219. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through www.moomedia.com.au.

What we collect

If you send us a message through our contact form or by email, we collect the details you choose to give us – usually your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and performance purposes.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, provide the services you ask for, and keep in touch about work we are doing together. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Contact form messages are delivered to us by a third-party form service, and the website is hosted by Cloudflare. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Australia. Embedded videos are provided by YouTube and Vimeo, who may set their own cookies when you play a video.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Access and corrections

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing admin@moomedia.com.au.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

Let’s work together
