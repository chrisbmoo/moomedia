moomedia specialises in video production from our studio in Geelong.

We have over 20 years design and media experience in the Geelong region and are proud to be part of our local community. We have completed hundreds of promotional videos for government, educational, corporate and not-for-profit organisations.

Digital communication via video content has never been more relevant or prolific and it is an outstanding medium to tell the story about your school to prospective parents and students.

Our schools package includes not only a unique promotional video filmed on site for your website but additional footage that can be used as content to promote the school via your social media and digital platforms over a full school year. It is a total video promotional package, fully edited.

For more information contact us today and we can help you create a unique video production that represents your organisation.
